# Planned 500-commit milestone

The planned Warka 500-commit development sequence was completed at commit 500
on 2026-10-01. The milestone records a broad implementation baseline rather
than a claim of external certification or production deployment.

The sequence established repository coverage for:

- education and school operations;
- student and guardian portals;
- academic setup, attendance, assessment, gradebook, and coursework workflows;
- documents, transfers, data quality, and regional reporting;
- governance, support, operations, security, recovery, and audit evidence;
- English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo, and calendar preferences;
- containers, deployment validation, reliability controls, runbooks, and
  release-readiness evidence.

Current environment integrations, usability improvements, manual accessibility
review, linguistic review, and target-deployment acceptance remain separate
work after this historical milestone.
