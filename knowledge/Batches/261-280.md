# Batch 261–280

Assessment administration reuses existing assessment definitions. Optional school rooms and schedules carry conflict validation for class, room, capacity, invigilation, active staff, and academic dates. An operational session and explicit participation are separate from a schedule and from marks. Missing participation is unrecorded, absence never creates zero, and a make-up request preserves the original status.

Gradebook entry windows use server time. Authorized overrides require a reason and audit. Completeness distinguishes missing, absent, pending make-up, invalid, and recorded zero. Moderation approval preserves the previous score in correction history; published results continue through the separate result correction workflow. Gradebook locks require complete entry and resolved moderation and remain separate from result approval and publication. Readiness reports reasons and never publishes automatically.

Staff controls use current school scope and exact teacher assignment. Student progress uses linked account access; guardian progress uses a verified eligible child and school. Summaries report published results and recorded assessment activity without rankings, predictive failure scores, or student risk scoring.

Focused tests and midpoint validation passed during the batch. Local PostgreSQL and live Playwright execution were unavailable; final CI provides the database-backed validation. See [[Testing]] and [[KnownIssues]] for limits.
