# Batch 281–300

Coursework assignments are separate from official assessment definitions. A teacher may explicitly link an assignment to an existing assessment in the same school, year, period, class, and subject. The audience derives from current approved enrollment. Draft assignments are private; publication requires clean scans for active assignment files. Submission files can finish scanning after the student submits. The submitted time is immutable and does not depend on the scan.

Student submissions keep numbered revisions. A submitted revision is historical; returning work permits a new revision through a controlled deadline. Extensions record one student's effective due time without rewriting the assignment. Rubrics have ordered criteria, freeze after first scoring, and retain score versions. Plain-text teacher feedback remains private until release. A coursework score is never an official mark by itself. Explicit transfer calls the existing mark service under its entry-window, gradebook-lock, permission, range, duplicate, and published-result rules; provenance links the official mark to its revision and rubric score.

Verified guardians can read eligible published coursework, submission state, and released feedback and score. They cannot submit or change work. Coursework notifications use existing in-app, email, and digest preferences with neutral text and transition-based delivery. Student, guardian, and teacher summaries are factual counts only; there is no ranking, engagement score, or predictive risk model.

Focused service, API, and React checks ran during the batch. The browser journey requires PostgreSQL and controlled test scanning; local execution depends on a dedicated database. See [[Testing]] and [[KnownIssues]] for validation limits.
