# Warka user roles

- **Organization owner** — governs an organization, manages its school
  directory and organization-level access, and reviews operational controls.
- **School administrator** — manages school configuration, staff access,
  academic setup, and approval workflows within an assigned school.
- **Registrar** — maintains student records, registration, enrollment,
  transfers, corrections, and school documents.
- **Teacher** — works with assigned classes and subjects, including attendance,
  coursework, meetings, marks, and timetable information.
- **Student** — views the student's own authorized academic, attendance,
  coursework, document, and notification information.
- **Guardian** — views authorized linked-student information and participates
  in family communication, meetings, events, and permitted requests.
- **Bureau** — reviews purpose-limited regional reporting and submission
  evidence for the bureau's assigned scope.
- **Support** — handles support requests and uses explicitly approved,
  time-bounded support access where granted.
- **Operations** — monitors deployment, availability, communications, recovery,
  maintenance, incident, and other platform-operational evidence.

This reference summarizes responsibilities only. The implemented authorization
and membership rules remain the source of truth.
