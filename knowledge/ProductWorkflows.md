# Product workflow overview

## Student access

Registration creates the school student record. Enrollment places that student
in an academic year and class. Approved enrollment and teaching assignments
then determine the teacher's authorized class context; these steps do not grant
unrelated school access.

## Attendance visibility

An assigned teacher captures and finalizes attendance for an authorized class
session. Corrections retain history. Student and guardian views expose only the
attendance information permitted for the signed-in identity and linked student.

## Marks and publication

Teachers enter marks within their assignment and entry-window scope. Review,
moderation, completeness, and gradebook locks control progression. Approval and
explicit publication make results available to authorized student and family
views; draft work is not treated as published.

## Documents

An authorized user requests a school document. Staff review and issue it from a
controlled snapshot. Public verification exposes minimal verification facts,
while access to the private document remains separately authorized.

## School reporting

A school prepares a reporting snapshot, resolves readiness blockers, and
submits a version. Bureau reviewers work within their assigned scope and may
return, review, or accept submissions while preserving version and review
history.
