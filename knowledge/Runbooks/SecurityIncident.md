# SecurityIncident

## Purpose

Contain and investigate a security event.

## Preconditions

Restrict access, preserve logs, and use approved secure communication.

## Actions

Command: `pnpm security:check`

Run the command from the approved repository revision, record its safe summary, and perform only the explicitly authorized operational action.

## Verification

Verify factual health, audit evidence, affected queues, and the intended revision or state. Preserve the result without credentials or private records.

## Stop and escalate

Stop routine handling and escalate immediately for credential or private-data exposure.
