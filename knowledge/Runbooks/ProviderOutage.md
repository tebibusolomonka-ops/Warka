# ProviderOutage

## Purpose

Handle email, scanner, or storage provider degradation.

## Preconditions

Identify the provider and confirm dependent queues retain truthful states.

## Actions

Command: `pnpm readiness:check`

Run the command from the approved repository revision, record its safe summary, and perform only the explicitly authorized operational action.

## Verification

Verify factual health, audit evidence, affected queues, and the intended revision or state. Preserve the result without credentials or private records.

## Stop and escalate

Escalate when quarantined files could be released, delivery is ambiguous, or storage integrity is uncertain.
