# IncidentResponse

## Purpose

Coordinate a factual operational incident.

## Preconditions

Assign an operator, preserve current evidence, and identify affected services.

## Actions

Command: `pnpm drill:incident`

Run the command from the approved repository revision, record its safe summary, and perform only the explicitly authorized operational action.

## Verification

Verify factual health, audit evidence, affected queues, and the intended revision or state. Preserve the result without credentials or private records.

## Stop and escalate

Escalate on security or integrity impact, unknown blast radius, or unsafe mitigation.
